ABOUT MEDITRACK

Why we built this, and how we think about your data.

The Problem

Patients lose track of what they've taken and when. Old bills get thrown out, memory fades, and every new doctor starts from zero. MediTrack turns that scattered paper trail into one digital record that follows the patient, not the pharmacy.

Privacy & Trust

  • Your Patient ID alone never grants access to your history.
  • Share codes are temporary and expire automatically after 7 days.
  • Every access is logged, so you can see who viewed your data and when.
  • Codes can be revoked by you at any time, instantly.
